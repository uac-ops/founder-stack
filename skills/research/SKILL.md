# Research — keep listening to customers

`validate` was a one-time gate. `research` is the ongoing habit of learning from
users so the product keeps getting more right over time.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## 01 Idea`, `## 02 Validation`, `## 13 Support`
- Writes:   `### Research` (under Quality & Discovery), feeds `## 03 Plan`
- Agents:   `ux-researcher`
- Previous: any (continuous)
- Next:     feeds `plan` and `prototype`

## Goal
A steady flow of customer insight that turns into prioritized, evidence-backed
roadmap decisions.

## Inputs
- Open questions about users; support themes (`## 13 Support`); analytics.

## Process
1. **Pick one question.** Discovery is sharpest when aimed. e.g. "why do trial
   users not invite teammates?"
2. **Recruit the right people** with `ux-researcher` — actual target users, not
   whoever's easy. A handful of the right ones beats a survey of the wrong ones.
3. **Interview for behavior, not opinions.** Ask about the last time they faced
   the problem and what they actually did. Avoid "would you" hypotheticals.
4. **Find the job-to-be-done.** What progress are they trying to make? What did
   they "hire" your product (or a workaround) to do?
5. **Synthesize.** Cluster observations into patterns and surprises. Separate what
   users *said* from what they *did*.
6. **Turn insight into action.** Each strong pattern becomes a roadmap candidate
   in `## 03 Plan` or a design question for `prototype`.

## Exit criteria
- A clear answer (or sharper question) backed by real user input.
- Insights synthesized into roadmap candidates.

## Update the ledger
- Fill `### Research` (question, who, key findings, JTBD, actions).
- Feed candidates into `## 03 Plan`.
- Decision Log: `YYYY-MM-DD · research · learned <insight>`.
- Next Actions: the roadmap item or design test the research surfaced.

## Handoff
"You know more about your users. Route findings to `plan` (what to build) or
`prototype` (how to design it)."
