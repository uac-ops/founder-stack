# Sell — turn attention into revenue

A product with users and no revenue is a hobby. This stage prices the value,
builds the path to purchase, and closes.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## 02 Validation`, `## 11 Marketing`, `## 14 Finance`
- Writes:   `## 12 Sales`
- Agents:   `sales-closer`, `finance-clerk`
- Previous: `market`
- Next:     `support`

## Goal
A clear price and packaging, a working funnel, and a repeatable way to close.

## Inputs
- Pricing signal (`## 02 Validation`), traffic (`## 11 Marketing`), unit
  economics (`## 14 Finance`).

## Process
1. **Price on value, not cost.** With `sales-closer` + `finance-clerk`, anchor to
   the value delivered and the pricing signal from validation. Pick a model
   (subscription, one-time, usage). Don't underprice out of fear.
2. **Package into tiers.** Usually 2–3: a clear entry point and an obvious
   "most people pick this." Name the value, not the features.
3. **Build the funnel.** Landing → pricing → checkout. Remove friction: fewer
   fields, clear CTA, social proof, money-back confidence. Make the buy button
   obvious.
4. **Pick a motion** that fits the price:
   - **Self-serve** for low price — the product sells itself; optimize the page.
   - **Founder-led sales** for higher price — direct outreach and demos.
5. **Outreach (if founder-led).** Build a targeted list, write a short personal
   message about *their* problem, follow up, book calls, ask for the sale.
6. **Instrument conversion.** Track visit → trial → paid. Find the biggest
   drop-off and fix that one thing.

## Exit criteria
- Pricing and packaging live.
- A funnel that takes a visitor to payment.
- Conversion tracked; a first paying customer (or a concrete plan to get one).

## Update the ledger
- Fill `## 12 Sales` (pricing & packaging, funnel, outreach list, conversion notes).
- Decision Log: `YYYY-MM-DD · pricing = <model/price> · first paid = <y/n>`.
- Set `Stage: support`.
- Next Actions: "Run `support` to keep the customers you win."

## Handoff
"Money is flowing. Next run `support` so the customers you win actually stay."
