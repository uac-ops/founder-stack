# Launch — make a moment

A launch concentrates attention into a single day so a small product can punch
above its weight. This stage prepares the assets and runs the play.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## 01 Idea`, `## 04 Design`, `## 09 Docs`, `## 15 Legal`
- Writes:   `## 10 Launch`
- Agents:   `growth-marketer`
- Previous: `document`
- Next:     `market`

## Goal
A coordinated launch with assets ready, channels chosen, and results captured.

## Inputs
- The one-liner, design assets, live URL, and (required) finished `legal`.

## Before you start
Check that `## 01 Idea`, `## 04 Design`, `## 09 Docs`, `## 15 Legal` are filled in COMPANY.md. If a section you depend on is empty or still `_TBD_`, stop and tell the founder which skill fills it (see the orchestrator's stage table) instead of guessing.

## Process
1. **Pre-flight gate.** Confirm: product is live and stable, `legal` (ToS,
   Privacy) is done, and analytics work. Also confirm the quality passes are done
   — `accessibility`, `performance`, and `polish` — so the flood of launch
   traffic meets a product that's usable, fast, and considered. Don't launch onto
   a broken floor.
2. **Pick channels** with `growth-marketer`, matched to where your customers are
   (from `## 02 Validation`):
   - **Product Hunt** — schedule, prepare gallery, first comment, hunter.
   - **Hacker News (Show HN)** — honest title, a thoughtful first comment, be
     present to reply.
   - **Reddit** — the *specific* subreddit, follow its rules, give before you ask.
   - **X / LinkedIn** — a launch thread with a demo clip.
   - **Your list** — email the waitlist from `validate`.
3. **Build assets.** Tagline, 3–5 gallery images/GIF, a 30–60s demo, the launch
   copy per channel, and a pinned first comment that tells the origin story.
4. **Schedule.** Pick the day/time per channel norms. Line up a few friendly
   early supporters (no fake votes — just real people who'd genuinely care).
5. **Run the day.** Post, then *stay present*. Reply fast, thank people, fix
   small issues live.
6. **Capture results.** Traffic, signups, top feedback, what converted.

## Exit criteria
- All assets ready and channels scheduled.
- Launch executed with you present to engage.
- Results and feedback captured.
- Evidence shown for each criterion above (command output, screenshot, link, or measurement), not just asserted.

## Update the ledger
- Fill `## 10 Launch` (channels, assets, date, results).
- Decision Log: `YYYY-MM-DD · launched on <channels> · signups = <n>`.
- Set `Stage: market`.
- Next Actions: "Run `market` to turn the spike into a repeatable channel."

## Handoff
"The moment is spent. Next run `market` to build a channel that brings users
every week, not just launch day."
