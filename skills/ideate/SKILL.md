# Ideate — find the real problem

The goal of ideation is not to fall in love with a solution. It is to name a
problem worth solving and the riskiest belief underneath it.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## Meta`
- Writes:   `## 01 Idea`
- Agents:   `product-strategist` (optional, for sharpening)
- Previous: —
- Next:     `validate`

## Goal
A one-paragraph idea brief: the problem, who has it, your proposed solution, why
now, and the one assumption that most needs to be true.

## Inputs
- The founder's raw idea (ask for it if `## 01 Idea` is empty).

## Process
1. **Capture the raw idea** in the founder's own words.
2. **Interrogate the problem, not the solution.** Ask, one at a time:
   - Whose problem is this? Be specific — a person, not "businesses".
   - How do they solve it today? What does that cost them (time/money/pain)?
   - How often does the pain occur? Hair-on-fire or mild annoyance?
3. **Pressure-test desirability.** Would they pay, switch, or change behavior?
4. **Find the unfair advantage.** Why you, why now? Distribution, insight, speed.
5. **Name the riskiest assumption.** The one belief that, if false, kills it.
   This becomes the target of the next stage (`validate`).
6. **Write the one-liner:** "<product> helps <customer> <achieve outcome> by
   <approach>." Refine until it's specific and unembarrassing.

## Exit criteria
- A clear problem statement and named target customer.
- A one-liner you'd say out loud to a stranger.
- A single, testable riskiest assumption.

## Update the ledger
- Fill `## 01 Idea` (problem, target customer, solution, why now, riskiest
  assumption).
- Set `## Meta → One-liner`.
- Set `Stage: validate`.
- Decision Log: `YYYY-MM-DD · framed the idea · riskiest assumption = <X>`.
- Next Actions: "Run `validate` to test the riskiest assumption."

## Handoff
Tell the founder: the riskiest assumption is your test target. Next run
`validate` — do not start building yet.
