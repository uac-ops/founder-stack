# Market — build a repeatable channel

Launch day is a spike. Marketing is the system that brings users every week. The
solo-founder goal: find one channel that works and compound it.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## 01 Idea`, `## 02 Validation`, `## 10 Launch`
- Writes:   `## 11 Marketing`
- Agents:   `growth-marketer`
- Previous: `launch`
- Next:     `sell`

## Goal
Locked positioning and one repeatable channel producing a predictable flow of the
right visitors.

## Inputs
- Where customers hang out (`## 02 Validation`), launch learnings (`## 10`).

## Before you start
Check that `## 01 Idea`, `## 02 Validation`, `## 10 Launch` are filled in COMPANY.md. If a section you depend on is empty or still `_TBD_`, stop and tell the founder which skill fills it (see the orchestrator's stage table) instead of guessing.

## Process
1. **Lock positioning.** With `growth-marketer`, write the message house: who
   it's for, the problem, the promise, why you over alternatives. One sentence
   per row. Use it everywhere.
2. **Pick ONE primary channel** to go deep on (don't spread thin):
   - **Content/SEO** — rank for problems people search.
   - **Community** — be genuinely useful where customers gather.
   - **Social/build-in-public** — a consistent voice and cadence.
   - **Partnerships/integrations** — borrow others' audiences.
3. **SEO foundation** (even if not your main channel): keyword the core
   problems, write landing pages that match intent, fix titles/meta, ensure the
   site is fast and crawlable.
4. **Content calendar.** A realistic, sustainable cadence (e.g. one strong piece
   a week) tied to customer problems, not features.
5. **Capture and nurture.** Email capture + a simple welcome sequence so traffic
   doesn't leak away.
6. **Measure the funnel top.** Visitors → signups by source. Double down on what
   converts; cut what doesn't.

## Exit criteria
- Positioning/message house written and applied.
- One primary channel chosen with a content calendar.
- SEO basics in place; funnel-top metrics tracked.
- Evidence shown for each criterion above (command output, screenshot, link, or measurement), not just asserted.
