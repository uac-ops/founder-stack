# Support — keep customers happy

For a solo founder, support is not a cost center — it's your highest-bandwidth
research channel. Every ticket tells you what to fix and what to build.

## Connected skills
- Reads:    `## 09 Docs`, `## 12 Sales`
- Writes:   `## 13 Support`, (feeds) `## 03 Plan`
- Agents:   `support-rep`
- Previous: `sell`
- Next:     `operate`

## Goal
A sustainable support system that resolves issues fast and turns them into
product improvements — without eating your whole week.

## Inputs
- Docs (`## 09`) to deflect common questions; customers (`## 12 Sales`).

## Process
1. **Pick channels you can sustain.** One or two: email, a help widget, or a
   community. Set expectations (response time) you can actually meet solo.
2. **Deflect with docs.** With `support-rep`, turn the top questions into FAQ
   entries and in-product help so they stop arriving.
3. **Canned replies.** Draft warm, reusable responses for the common cases.
   Personalize the first line; keep the rest templated.
4. **Triage loop.** For each issue: acknowledge fast → resolve or work around →
   tag it (bug / confusion / feature / billing).
5. **Feed the roadmap.** Weekly, tally the tags. The biggest cluster is your next
   priority — push it into `## 03 Plan` for the next build loop.
6. **Close the loop.** Tell users when you ship the thing they asked for. It
   creates fans.

## Exit criteria
- Support channel(s) live with stated response times.
- FAQ + canned replies in place.
- A weekly triage habit that feeds the plan.

## Update the ledger
- Fill `## 13 Support` (channels, FAQ/canned replies, top issues).
- Decision Log: `YYYY-MM-DD · support live · top issue = <X>`.
- Set `Stage: operate`.
- Next Actions: "Run `operate` to set your north-star metric and automate the
  routine."

## Handoff
"Customers are cared for. Next run `operate` to put the whole machine on rails —
metrics, dashboards, and automations."
