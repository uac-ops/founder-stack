# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Founder Stack to be a welcoming place for everyone building things,
regardless of experience level or background. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what's best for the community and for solo founders using this.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily
or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer via the GitHub profile
contact or a private security advisory. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
